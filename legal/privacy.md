# IronSight Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-07-19

## Data we collect
- Account: email, name, password hash, plan, Stripe customer id
- Usage: sessions created, job history, support tickets
- Content you upload: videos, derived frames, audio, 3D assets, annotations
- Technical: IP for rate limiting, basic request logs

## How we use data
To run the product (processing pipelines, billing, support, abuse prevention, product improvement). We do not sell personal data.

## Processors
- Stripe (payments)
- OpenRouter / vision providers (frame analysis when configured)
- Hosting / tunnel providers you deploy with

## Retention
Session media may be retained per operator policy (default 90 days configurable). Account data retained while active; deleted on account deletion subject to legal holds and backups.

## Your rights
Access, correction, export of wrap/session JSON, and account deletion via the app or support.

## Security
Passwords hashed (PBKDF2). JWT sessions. Multi-tenant session isolation by owner/org. Operators should use HTTPS and restricted host access.

## Contact
Use in-app Support for privacy requests.
