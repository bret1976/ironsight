# IronSight Refund & Beta Accuracy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-07-19

## Subscriptions
Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime via **Billing** (Stripe Customer Portal). Access continues through the paid period.

## Refunds
- Within **14 days** of first paid charge, if you have processed fewer than 5 paid sessions, contact support for a full refund.
- After that, refunds are discretionary for service outages longer than 48 hours caused by us.

## Accuracy disclaimer (important)
IronSight scores shots with AI **assist**. UNKNOWN / needs-review is expected on difficult footage.  
**Coach corrections are the source of truth.** We do not refund solely because AI disagreed with a human call when review tools were available.

## Contact
Open a Support ticket in the app with your account email and Stripe receipt id.
